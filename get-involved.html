Colony caregiver

The card.

Applying costs ₹50 and the card is not issued on the spot: a named person at PFA reads the application and decides. You get an application number the moment the fee clears.

Feeding community dogs is lawful. The ABC Rules 2023 recognise caregivers, and the courts have held that a resident association cannot stop it.

The card is a document you can show when that is disputed. It is not a permit, and it does not give PFA any authority over your colony: it records that PFA knows who you are and what you do.

The fifty rupees confirms the application and gives it a number you can track it with. It is not payment for a card: a named person at PFA reads every application and decides. If it is not approved, the fee is not refunded, because it pays for the reading rather than the card. Every application appears in PFA's admin record under Caregiver Cards.
