Her, in motion

PFA units

Someone near you is already doing this.

The aim is an animal welfare centre in every one of India’s roughly 800 districts. Every unit is run by people who live in the place it covers, with a name, a number and an address attached.

Each week this space will carry one unit telling its own story, in a film by the people who run it. The first films from unit heads are on their way; until then, every unit is a search away.
