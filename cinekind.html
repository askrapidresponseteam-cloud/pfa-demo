The honours

Ten awards, one question.

Was this film kind to the animals in it, and did it leave the country a little gentler?

01
The CineKind Compassion AwardThe highest honour, for a lifetime of widening the circle.
02
Director of ChangeFor direction that led with compassion.
03
Actor for KindnessFor lending fame to animals.
04
Cinematic Impact for Animal WelfareFor a film that changed something real.
05
Kindness in FrameFor one unforgettable scene.
06
Voice for the VoicelessFor writing and sound that spoke for animals.
07
Innovation for CompassionFor craft that kept a real animal out of harm.
08
Visual Storyteller for AnimalsFor filming animals at their own eye level.
09
Guardians of KindnessFor crews who protected every life on set.
10
Humane InfluenceFor carrying kindness beyond cinema.

Each trophy is a sculpture by Paresh Maity. Winners are chosen by the CineKind National Awards Committee, chaired by Dr Sandeep Marwah.
