Plan a campaign

Start something for the animals.

A birthday, a workplace drive, a run, a school collection. Pick a cause and a way to raise for it, set a goal and watch the campaign take shape. Then send the plan to PFA, who will reply on how to run it well.

Causes

Six causes to raise for.

Choose one and the plan below starts from it.

  • Cows in need

    Cows abandoned when they stop giving milk wait on the streets: many old, injured or hungry. PFA's Gau Seva campaign raised for their care; yours can carry it on.

  • Community dogs

    Sterilisation and vaccination for the dogs of a neighbourhood, the humane way to fewer puppies on the street and no rabies.

  • Injured and sick animals

    Treatment for the animals hit by traffic, hurt by people or simply ill, who have no one to pay for it.

  • Feeding drives

    Food for the animals that community feeders look after, when one person's money no longer stretches.

  • The animal care centre

    The Sanjay Gandhi Animal Care Centre in Delhi and the thousands of animals in its care at any time.

  • Where it is needed most

    Let PFA put what you raise where it will do the most good at the time.

Ways to run one

Pick the one that suits you.

  • A birthday or celebration

    Ask the people coming to give to the animals instead of bringing a present.

  • A workplace drive

    Colleagues give for a month, and the company may match what they raise.

  • A school or college drive

    A class, a house or a whole campus. For something that lasts, see PFA Campus.

  • A run, walk or challenge

    Friends sponsor your distance, your match or a challenge you set yourself.

  • A collection of supplies

    Food, blankets and medicines, collected in your building, office or temple.

  • An awareness event

    A talk, a screening, a stall at a fair: raising voices, not only money.

What works

Four things good campaigns share.

  • 01

    One clear cause

    Nine dogs on one street moves people more than all the dogs everywhere.

  • 02

    A deadline

    A birthday, a month, a race day. People give when there is a date.

  • 03

    A picture

    One honest photograph of the animal or the place, with a line about why it matters to you.

  • 04

    A thank-you

    Tell everyone who gave what their money did. It is how the next campaign starts.

Questions

Before you begin.

Can I collect money myself?

Please do not collect money in PFA's name until PFA has replied to your plan. Donations made through PFA's own donate page are receipted by PFA, and donations to People for Animals are eligible for tax deduction under Section 80G.

Will PFA give me a page, a poster or a letter?

Say what you need in your plan. The reply will tell you what PFA can provide for it.

Can I collect supplies instead of money?

Yes. Choose "Supplies" as the goal and say what you will collect; PFA will tell you where they are needed and how to get them there.

What about a corporate campaign?

A drive among colleagues can be planned here. For a company's CSR, see CSR partnerships.
