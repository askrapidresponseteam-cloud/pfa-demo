## v1.324

- **Pure white, actually verified.** Three faults from v1.321-322,
  fixed at the root and then audited rather than asserted. One: 115
  rules of light text had survived the theme change on now-light
  grounds - selectors outside the converted sections' prefixes, like
  the units gallery's bio, which read as invisible until selected. A
  page-by-page sweep flipped every light-text rule that does not sit
  on its own dark chip or over imagery, with three casualties of the
  sweep itself (the deck's close glyph, the film number badge, the
  achievements lightbox) mended by hand. Two: the grounds go from
  stone to pure white as instructed - the stone token is paper now on
  every page, the hero washes that read as smudges on white are gone,
  and submission-collage, a page-black straggler, is converted. Three:
  the titles are now actually cut to the chase - the heroes' reserved
  half-screen is removed, headlines drop to a working scale, and
  eyebrows that repeated the headline are gone (the search page's
  status line, which only rested on the same words, is restored with
  a note). The final audit is mechanical and printed: zero
  light-on-light rules, zero dark-on-dark rules, both directions,
  every page, and 624 tests.
