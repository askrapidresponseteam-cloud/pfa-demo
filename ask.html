Ask us anything

A question? Ask a person.

About an animal, a law, a unit near you, adopting, feeding a colony, volunteering, or something we have not thought of. It goes to a named person at PFA and you get a reference so it cannot be lost.

Before you ask

  1. An animal is being hurt right nowThat is not a question, it is a report. Report cruelty goes to the front of the queue.
  2. An injured animal in front of youFind the nearest PFA unit and call them. Ask here afterwards if you still need to.
  3. A law questionMany are already answered, each citing the section it rests on. Animal laws in India. If yours is not there, ask below and we will add it.

Answers come by email or a call on the mobile you give. Most are answered within a few working days; a question that turns out to be urgent is moved to the front.
