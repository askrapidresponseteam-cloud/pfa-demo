Where has it got to?

One place for anything you sent PFA: a cruelty report, a question, a volunteer or caregiver application, a job application, a film for the wall, or a shop order. You need the number you were given and the email or mobile you gave with it.

Whatever PFA gave you when you sent it. Case does not matter.
It has to be the one on the record, so that only you can see it.

    Lost the number?

    It is in the acknowledgement PFA sent to the email or mobile you gave. If you cannot find it, ask us with the same contact and a named person will look it up.
