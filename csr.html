Corporate partnerships

CSR that reaches the animals.

Animal welfare is named in Schedule VII of the Companies Act, 2013, among the activities a company's CSR can fund. Here are twelve programmes PFA can build with a company, each one meant to leave something standing: a town where dogs are sterilised, a rescue centre, a vet trained for a village.

Programmes

Twelve programmes, each built to last.

Filter by where the work happens. Add the ones your company would consider; they go straight into the brief below.

  • Villages and farms

    Rural animal hospitals

    Build or run a hospital for farm animals in a district where none is within reach.

  • Villages and farms

    Village veterinary compounders

    Train young people as veterinary compounders who go back to work in their own villages.

  • Villages and farms

    Organic farming workshops

    Teach farmers organic methods that keep their animals, their soil and their water healthy.

  • Working animals

    Better carts

    Redesign animal carts so the oxen that pull them stop suffering neck injuries.

  • Towns and cities

    Sterilisation for a town

    Fund dog sterilisation and vaccination in smaller towns, bringing down rabies and bites humanely.

  • Towns and cities

    A shelter for a city

    One shelter and treatment centre for a city that has none, to care for its animals.

  • Wildlife

    Fruit trees by the forest

    Plant fruit trees along forest edges, so monkeys find food there instead of coming into towns.

  • Wildlife

    Wildlife rescue centres

    Rescue and care centres for elephants, injured birds and leopards, and campaigns for species in trouble, like the sparrow.

  • Wildlife

    Turtle nesting sites

    Protect the beaches where turtles nest, and with them the rivers and the coast.

  • Wildlife

    Stopping poachers

    Fund the units that catch wildlife poachers and the people who trade in what they take.

  • Disasters

    Floods and cyclones

    Fund the rescue and care of animals when floods and cyclones strike, when they are the last to be thought of.

  • Training and teaching

    Veterinary teaching

    Veterinary teaching that covers wildlife, zoo animals, birds and small animals, not only livestock.

Why animal welfare

A cause that reaches every community.

  • 01

    It is in the law

    Schedule VII of the Companies Act, 2013 lists animal welfare among the activities CSR may fund, beside environmental sustainability and the protection of flora and fauna.

  • 02

    It touches every life

    Rabies, road safety, livelihoods that depend on working and farm animals, and the health of rivers and forests all run through how animals are treated.

  • 03

    You can see it

    A sterilised town, a rescue centre, a trained vet: work your people can visit, and outcomes your board can read.

How it works

From a first note to an agreed plan.

  1. Send a briefThe areas you would fund, the shape and the budget, in the form below. Two minutes.
  2. Talk it throughA named person at PFA writes back to understand what your company wants the money to change.
  3. Agree a planA plan with its costs, its place and what it will achieve, for your CSR committee to approve.
  4. See it happenThe work, reported against what you agreed to track, and open to your people to visit.

Questions

What CSR teams ask.

Is animal welfare an eligible CSR activity?

Yes. Item (iv) of Schedule VII of the Companies Act, 2013 includes animal welfare, alongside environmental sustainability, ecological balance and the protection of flora and fauna. Your CSR committee decides how it applies to your policy.

Can we fund something that is not on the list?

Yes. The twelve are where PFA would start; say what you have in mind in the brief, or choose "Open to suggestion".

Can our people volunteer?

Employee volunteering can be part of any partnership. Tell PFA in the brief, and say where your offices are.

What documents will you share?

Ask in your brief for what your CSR team needs for due diligence, and PFA will tell you what it can provide.
